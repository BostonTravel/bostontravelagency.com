Plan around the big days

Boston events calendar

Boston's calendar is packed with traditions, from the Marathon on Patriots' Day to the feasts of the North End and First Night on New Year's Eve. Here are the events we think are worth planning a trip around, listed in order of what's coming up next.

Where organizers have announced dates, we show them. Otherwise we show the date the event usually falls on, so check the official site before you book flights or hotels.

Photo: Bill Morrow, CC BY 2.0

Oct·

Salem Haunted Happenings

All of October
Salem · Typical timing

A month of Halloween parades, haunted tours and costume events in the Witch City. Take the ferry from Boston and skip the traffic.

Pair it with: Salem High-Speed Ferry from Boston →Official site
Dec3

Boston Common Tree Lighting

Likely Thu, Dec 3, 2026
Boston Common · Typical timing: Usually the first Thursday in December

The city lights the tree sent each year by Nova Scotia as thanks for Boston's help after the 1917 Halifax Explosion.

Official site
Dec16

Boston Tea Party Reenactment

Likely Wed, Dec 16, 2026
Old South Meeting House and the waterfront · Typical timing: On or around December 16

Join the debate at Old South Meeting House, then follow the 'Sons of Liberty' to the harbor as the tea goes overboard.

Pair it with: Boston Tea Party Ships & Museum →Official site
Dec31

First Night Boston

Thu, Dec 31, 2026
City Hall Plaza, Copley Square and the waterfront · Annual date

America's original First Night, running since 1976: a parade, ice sculptures, music across the city and fireworks over Boston Harbor at midnight. Free, and the T is free in the evening.

Official site
Feb·

Lunar New Year in Chinatown

Late January or February, following the lunar calendar
Chinatown · Typical timing

Lion dances wind through Chinatown's streets with drums, firecrackers and crowds, usually on the weekend after the new year begins.

Official site
Mar14

St. Patrick's Day Parade

Likely Sun, Mar 14, 2027
South Boston · Typical timing: The Sunday closest to March 17

One of the biggest St. Patrick's Day parades in the country, marching through Southie with bagpipers, bands and a very festive crowd.

Pair it with: Freedom Trail History Pub Crawl →Official site
Apr·

Red Sox Opening Day at Fenway

Late March or early April; see the Red Sox schedule
Fenway Park · Typical timing

The first home game at the oldest ballpark in the major leagues is a Boston holiday in all but name. Tickets go fast.

Pair it with: Tour of Historic Fenway Park →Official site
Apr19

Patriots' Day Reenactments

Mon, Apr 19, 2027
Lexington and Concord · Annual date

At dawn on Lexington Green, reenactors restage the first shots of the Revolution, followed by battles and parades along the Battle Road to Concord.

Pair it with: Private Day Trip to Lexington and Concord →Official site
Apr19

Boston Marathon

Mon, Apr 19, 2027
Hopkinton to Boylston Street · Annual date

The world's oldest annual marathon. Watch the finish on Boylston Street or the climb up Heartbreak Hill in Newton. Book hotels early; the city fills up.

Official site
May28

Boston Calling Music Festival

Likely May 28 to 30, 2027
Harvard Athletic Complex, Allston · Typical timing: Memorial Day weekend, Friday to Sunday

Three days of big-name bands and local favorites across several stages, kicking off Boston's summer.

Official site
Jul4

Fourth of July and the Boston Pops Fireworks

Sun, Jul 4, 2027
Charles River Esplanade · Annual date

The Boston Pops play on the Esplanade before fireworks over the Charles. Harborfest fills the waterfront with reenactments and the USS Constitution turnaround cruise.

Pair it with: Boston Harbor Sunset Cruise →Official site
Jul23

International Sand Sculpting Festival

Jul 23 to 25, 2027 Dates confirmed
Revere Beach · Confirmed

Master sand sculptors from around the world compete on America's first public beach, with food, music and fireworks. Free to attend and a short Blue Line ride from downtown.

Official site
Aug12

Fisherman's Feast

Likely Aug 12 to 15, 2027
North End · Typical timing: Mid-August, Thursday to Sunday

Boston's oldest Italian feast, honoring the Madonna del Soccorso. Don't miss the Flight of the Angel on Sunday, when a child is lowered over North Street.

Pair it with: North End Food Tour: Pizza, Pasta and Pastries →Official site
Aug27

Saint Anthony's Feast

Likely Aug 27 to 29, 2027
North End · Typical timing: The last full weekend of August

The largest Italian religious festival in New England: a 10-hour procession, street food, music and the whole neighborhood out on the sidewalks.

Pair it with: North End Food Tour with Wine and Limoncello →Official site
Oct15

Head of the Charles Regatta

Likely Oct 15 to 17, 2027
Charles River · Typical timing: The weekend of the third Sunday in October

The world's largest three-day rowing event. Spread a blanket on the riverbank near the Weeks or Eliot bridges and watch thousands of crews race past.

Pair it with: Boston Duck Boat Tour with Charles River Splash →Official site

Planning tips

  • Hotels sell out and prices jump on Marathon weekend, Head of the Charles weekend and college graduation weekends in May. Book as early as you can.
  • For July 4th and First Night, take the T; streets near the Esplanade and the waterfront close to traffic.
  • The North End feasts are free. Go hungry and bring cash for the street vendors.

Dates marked "likely" follow each event's usual pattern and may change. Always confirm on the official site before you book. Last updated October 2026.
